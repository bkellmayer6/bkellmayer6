# 💫 About Me:
I'm Bennett Kellmayer, a graduate student studying geodetic science at The Ohio State University under the advisement of Dr. Demián Gómez. I earned a Bachelor of Science in Geology from the University of Maryland, College Park. I am passionate about using geodetic and geophysical data to understand our natural world! My current research utilizes continuous GNSS networks and satellite radar observations to model postseismic deformation following megathrust earthquakes.

📫 Reach me at: kellmayer.1[at]osu.edu or connect with me on LinkedIn!
